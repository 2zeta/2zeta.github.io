I am also interested in IT certifications and professional training programs. Through self-learning, technical projects, and continuous practice, I aim to build strong expertise across Web Development, Networking, and Cybersecurity while preparing for future professional opportunities in the technology sector.

My objective is to become a versatile IT professional capable of designing web applications, managing secure networks, solving technical challenges, and contributing to innovative technology projects at an international level.
